01 / THE FINDING

Less friction. More intention.

Teams that used a focused workspace were 38% more likely to return to their core task after an interruption. A quieter interface appears to support deeper, more sustained work.

We observed 24 product teams over six weeks. Their workflows varied, but a common pattern emerged: when the next step was clear, people spent less time negotiating with the tool.

“The best interface is the one that leaves you with a clearer thought.”— Interview participant, design lead
